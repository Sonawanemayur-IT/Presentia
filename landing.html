Features

Everything You Need to Manage Attendance

Presentia combines powerful analytics with an intuitive interface that students, faculty, and admins love.

Real-Time Dashboard

Beautiful, intuitive dashboards give students, faculty, and administrators instant visibility into attendance metrics, trends, and potential issues.

Smart Shortage Alerts

Automatic notifications when any student's attendance drops below the 75% threshold. Proactive alerts prevent academic issues before they arise.

Multi-Role Access

Tailored experiences for students, faculty, and administrators. Each role sees exactly what they need — no more, no less.

Bulk Attendance Operations

Mark all present or absent with one click, then toggle individual records. Save hours of manual data entry every week.

Detailed Subject Analytics

Track attendance per subject with visual progress bars, color-coded health badges (Safe, At Risk, Critical), and historical trends.

Browser-Based Privacy

All data stays securely in your browser with IndexedDB storage. No cloud uploads, no third-party servers, complete institutional control.
